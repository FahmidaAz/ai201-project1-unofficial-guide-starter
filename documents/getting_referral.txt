HOW TO GET A REFERRAL AT A TECH COMPANY — Insider Advice

WHY REFERRALS MATTER
At big tech companies, referred candidates are 5-10x more likely to get an interview than cold applicants. Referrals skip the ATS filter and often go directly to a recruiter or hiring manager. If you know someone at your target company, a referral is worth more than any resume optimization.

WHO CAN REFER YOU?
Any employee at the company can submit a referral — it doesn't have to be someone in your target team or even your target role. The referral just gets your resume in front of a recruiter with a stamp of trust. Even a junior engineer's referral carries weight.

HOW TO ASK FOR A REFERRAL — THE RIGHT WAY
Bad: "Hey, can you refer me to Google?"
Good: "Hi [Name], I came across your profile and saw you work at Google as a [role]. I'm a [your background] currently applying for the [specific role] on the [team] team. I'd love to connect and hear about your experience there. Would you be open to a 15-minute chat?"

Always:
- Be specific about the role you want
- Show you've done research
- Ask to connect first, not for the referral immediately
- Make it easy for them — offer to send your resume and the job link

WHERE TO FIND PEOPLE TO ASK
1. LinkedIn — search "[Company] [Your School]" to find alumni
2. CodePath alumni network
3. Former classmates and professors
4. Hackathon connections
5. Tech Twitter/X community
6. Meetup.com tech events

COLD OUTREACH ON LINKEDIN
Connection rate message (under 300 characters):
"Hi [Name]! I'm a [role] interested in [Company]. I noticed you work there and went to [shared school/bootcamp]. Would love to connect and learn about your experience."

Follow-up after connecting:
"Thanks for connecting! I'm currently applying for [role] at [Company] and would love to hear about your experience there. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute chat this week or next?"

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THEY AGREE
1. Send them your resume (PDF)
2. Send the exact job link
3. Write a 2-3 sentence summary of why you're a fit
4. Make it zero effort for them — they just click "submit referral"

REFERRAL ETIQUETTE
- Don't ask someone you've never spoken to for a referral immediately
- Don't ask multiple employees at the same company simultaneously
- Always thank them regardless of outcome
- Update them when you hear back — they stuck their neck out for you
- If you get the job, buy them coffee or send a thoughtful thank you

WHEN YOU DON'T KNOW ANYONE
1. Apply cold AND work on getting a referral simultaneously
2. Attend virtual and in-person company info sessions
3. Engage with engineers on LinkedIn — comment thoughtfully on their posts
4. Contribute to open source projects that company engineers maintain
5. Reach out to recruiters directly on LinkedIn

REFERRAL BONUS CONTEXT
Most tech companies pay employees $2,000-$10,000 for referrals that get hired. Employees are often motivated to help — it's good for them too if you're qualified.
