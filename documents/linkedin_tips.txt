LINKEDIN PROFILE OPTIMIZATION FOR TECH JOB SEEKERS

WHY LINKEDIN MATTERS
Recruiters at top tech companies search LinkedIn daily for candidates. A strong profile means inbound recruiter messages — you get opportunities without applying. A weak profile means you're invisible. It takes 2 hours to optimize and pays off for years.

PROFILE PHOTO
Use a clear, professional headshot. Plain background, good lighting, smile. No group photos, no sunglasses, no filters. You don't need a professional photographer — a well-lit selfie works fine.

HEADLINE — MOST IMPORTANT FIELD
Don't just put your job title. Pack it with keywords recruiters search for.
Bad: "Student at NYU"
Good: "Software Engineer | Python • React • AWS | Open to New Opportunities"
Bad: "Looking for opportunities"
Good: "Full Stack Developer | Node.js • PostgreSQL • Docker | CodePath Grad"

ABOUT SECTION
Write 3-4 sentences in first person:
- Who you are and what you build
- Your technical strengths
- What kind of role you're looking for
- One personal touch (why you love engineering)
End with: "Feel free to reach out at [email]"

EXPERIENCE SECTION
For each role, write 3-5 bullet points using the same format as your resume:
Action verb + what you did + measurable result
"Reduced API response time by 35% by implementing Redis caching layer"
"Built automated test suite covering 85% of codebase, reducing bug rate by 40%"

SKILLS SECTION
Add at least 20 skills. LinkedIn's algorithm ranks profiles with more skills higher. Ask 3-5 connections to endorse your top skills — this boosts visibility significantly. Skills to include: your languages, frameworks, tools, soft skills (communication, leadership).

PROJECTS SECTION
Add your top 2-3 projects with:
- Project name and brief description
- Technologies used
- Link to GitHub or live demo
This is crucial for new grads and bootcamp graduates.

EDUCATION SECTION
Include your degree AND any bootcamps, certifications, or online courses. CodePath, freeCodeCamp, Google certificates all belong here.

OPEN TO WORK SETTINGS
Turn on "Open to Work" (you can choose to show it only to recruiters, not publicly). Set your preferences: job titles, locations, job types (full-time, internship), start date. The more specific, the better matches you get.

GROWING YOUR NETWORK STRATEGICALLY
- Connect with everyone you meet at hackathons, meetups, classes
- Connect with all your classmates and professors
- Connect with engineers at target companies (with a personalized note)
- Connect with recruiters at companies you want to work at
- Target: 500+ connections to unlock full LinkedIn search visibility

CONTENT AND ENGAGEMENT
Posting content dramatically increases profile views:
- Share a project you built (with screenshots)
- Write about something you learned
- Comment thoughtfully on posts by engineers you admire
Even 1-2 posts per month helps. Recruiters notice active profiles.

LINKEDIN PREMIUM
Not worth paying for as a job seeker. The free version with "Open to Work" enabled is sufficient. InMail credits matter more for recruiters than candidates.

MESSAGES FROM RECRUITERS — HOW TO RESPOND
Even if you're not interested: "Thanks for reaching out! I'm not actively looking right now but I'd love to stay connected for future opportunities."
If interested: "Thanks for reaching out! I'd love to learn more. What does the role involve and what's the comp range?"
